## Banned defaults (template tells)
Never use these unless the brief explicitly asks for them:
- Untouched shadcn look: slate/zinc greys + default blue/black primary, one radius everywhere,
  identical cards with the same soft shadow.
- Inter/system font as the brand face with no other typographic decision.
- Purple→blue or rainbow gradient washes as decoration; glassmorphism by default.
- Warm cream background + serif display + terracotta accent; near-black + one acid accent.
- Hero = big headline with one word in a different color + two buttons + a screenshot in a tilted
  frame; then three identical feature cards with icons.
- ALL-CAPS tracked eyebrow labels above every heading; `A · B · C` meta strings; `→` on every link
  or button; numbered `01 / 02 / 03` markers on content that is not a sequence.
- Fade-and-slide-up on every section, hover lift on every card.
- Emoji as icons; lucide icons dropped in everywhere as decoration.
- Lorem ipsum or generic copy ("Supercharge your workflow", "All-in-one platform").

## Required in every project
- A **concept** grounded in the product's subject (its materials, culture, vernacular, users),
  written in one sentence.
- A **signature element** — one memorable thing (type treatment, shape language, color moment,
  illustration style, motion moment); everything else stays quiet.
- A deliberate **type pairing** with a real scale, tested in every locale of the product
  (ru/uz/en: Cyrillic and Latin).
- **Hierarchy through contrast** of size, weight, color, and space — not through boxes and borders.
- **Real content** in the product's language: real-looking names, prices (UZS etc.), dates.
- Light and dark themes both designed, both AA.
